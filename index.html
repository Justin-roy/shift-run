SHIFT RUN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game SHIFT RUN (package com.shiftrun.app, "the Game", "we") handles information. We keep it short because the Game collects very little.

1. Information the Game itself stores

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it has no user accounts. Your progress (levels completed, stars, scores, coins, unlocked heroes and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating-system version, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data such as ad views and taps, plus diagnostic and crash information from the SDK.

Google may use this information to show personalised ads where you have consented and where the law allows. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it's required): on first launch the Game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy rights: where applicable, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the same consent and device settings.

4. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not run servers that receive data from the Game. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled and retained under Google's policies and is encrypted in transit.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
